## The Sensor Size vs. Weight Tradeoff

This is the central decision. Here's the quantified tradeoff:

| Factor | MFT (2x crop) | APS-C (1.5x crop) | Full Frame |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|------------|
| Typical body weight | 340-660g | 440-660g | 460-910g |
| 85mm-equiv prime weight | 116-425g | 445g | 470-1,195g |
| 24-70mm-equiv f/2.8 zoom weight | 254-382g | 410-494g | 695-935g |
| High-ISO noise (relative) | 2 stops behind FF | 1 stop behind FF | Reference |
| Max background blur (85mm equiv) | ~f/2.4 FF equiv (at f/1.2) | ~f/1.8 FF equiv (at f/1.2) | f/1.2-f/1.4 |
| Lightest portrait kit | ~500g | ~650g | ~620g* |

*Canon R8 + RF 50mm f/1.8 is exceptionally light for FF, but lacks IBIS and the lens is ~80mm equiv, not 85mm.

**For people photography in decent light:** MFT or APS-C deliver excellent results at dramatically lower weight. The noise difference is negligible below ISO 1600.

**For low-light or maximum background separation:** FF has a clear physics advantage.

## Recommendations by Priority

### If weight is the #1 priority:
**OM System OM-1 II + M.Zuiko 12-40mm f/2.8 PRO II + 45mm f/1.8**
- Total: ~1,097g for a flagship body, pro zoom, and portrait prime
- 120 fps burst, 8.5-stop IBIS, IP53 weather sealing
- Cost: ~$3,350

### If weight matters but you want more background blur:
**Fujifilm X-T5 + XF 56mm f/1.2 R WR + XF 16-55mm f/2.8 II**
- Total: ~1,452g for two-lens portrait kit
- f/1.2 gives ~f/1.8 FF-equivalent DoF
- Cost: ~$3,900

### If AF quality is the #1 priority:
**Sony A7C II + FE 85mm f/1.4 GM II + FE 24-70mm f/2.8 GM II**
- Total: ~1,851g for the best AF system + exceptional glass
- Lightest FF body (514g) keeps weight reasonable
- Cost: ~$6,100

### If budget is the #1 priority:
**OM System E-M10 IV + M.Zuiko 45mm f/1.8 + 12-45mm f/4 PRO**
- Total: ~753g — featherweight
- Cost: ~$1,450
- Trade-off: contrast-detect AF only (slower tracking)

### Best balance of weight, quality, and cost:
**Panasonic G9 II + 42.5mm f/1.7 + 12-35mm f/2.8 II**
- Total: ~1,093g for phase-detect AF, 60fps burst, pro zoom + portrait prime
- MFT weight with modern AF
- Cost: ~$3,100
